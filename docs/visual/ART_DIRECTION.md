# VELA art direction and result

Three words: open, assured, optimistic. Original graphic direction, no borrowed website composition or raster references. DESIGN.md records the considered alternatives and chosen palette/type system.

The hero uses an intentional two-card offset over a citron disk, nested linework and a small transaction tile. One flat paper ground, deep-green text and thin dividers keep the visual emphasis on the headline and art. The transition to pale sage opens the statement; the product section returns to paper; the closing citron area resolves the opening accent. A large footer wordmark closes the page.

Artwork is DOM/CSS/inline SVG; there is no renderer, simulated world, model, stock image or inherited Three.js look-dev requirement. Hero and section captures in captures/review-2026-09-27 were inspected as images. Captures are sampled states, not continuous viewing.
